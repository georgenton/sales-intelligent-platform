# Sales Intelligence Platform

## Phase 1 Demo Script

Target duration: 15–20 minutes. Use the staging tenant and existing synthetic demo data. Do not create, edit, import, review or delete records during the stakeholder presentation.

### 1. Problem today — 1 minute

- **What to click:** Open the staging login page; do not sign in yet.
- **What to explain:** Pipeline, forecast, quota and review evidence currently tend to live in disconnected spreadsheets and conversations.
- **Business problem solved:** Establishes one governed commercial view for sellers and managers.
- **Do not overclaim:** This is not yet a full CRM or a replacement for every operational system.

### 2. Manager Command Center — 1.5 minutes

- **What to click:** Sign in as Manager and open Command Center.
- **What to explain:** The page begins with the management question: will the team reach quota and where is intervention required?
- **Business problem solved:** Replaces manual assembly of the weekly commercial position.
- **Do not overclaim:** Values depend on configured quota and maintained opportunity/billing facts.

### 3. Quarter, quota, billed, forecast and gap — 1.5 minutes

- **What to click:** Point to the current fiscal period and the executive metric cards.
- **What to explain:** Quota is the target; Billed is actual revenue; Open forecast is current-quarter Best Case plus Commit; Projected gap is quota minus billed minus open forecast.
- **Business problem solved:** Makes the quarter position and remaining exposure immediately understandable.
- **Do not overclaim:** Missing configuration is shown as unavailable; it is not inferred or converted into zero.

### 4. Brand performance — 1 minute

- **What to click:** Scroll to the brand-performance table.
- **What to explain:** Compare quota, billed, forecast, Commit, projected gap, attainment and GM by brand.
- **Business problem solved:** Shows which vendors or portfolios are ahead, behind or margin-sensitive.
- **Do not overclaim:** Brand results are only as complete as the configured quotas and source attribution.

### 5. Sales Funnel — 1 minute

- **What to click:** Expand one funnel stage inline.
- **What to explain:** Stages narrow from 20 through 90; 100 is terminal billed revenue and is not an open funnel stage.
- **Business problem solved:** Makes volume, value and risk by commercial maturity visible.
- **Do not overclaim:** Funnel stage is governed commercial evidence, not a predictive score.

### 6. Open one opportunity — 1 minute

- **What to click:** Open an existing opportunity from the expanded funnel; close the drawer with Escape afterward.
- **What to explain:** Customer, seller, amount, stage, forecast, next action, evidence, dates, margin, history and alerts remain in context.
- **Business problem solved:** Managers can investigate a deal without losing the dashboard.
- **Do not overclaim:** Do not edit the record during the review.

### 7. Gross margin — 1 minute

- **What to click:** Point to GM and line-item economics in the opportunity drawer.
- **What to explain:** Sellers enter GM %; the platform derives gross profit and rejects inconsistent financial combinations.
- **Business problem solved:** Adds commercial-quality and profitability context to revenue discussions.
- **Do not overclaim:** The platform does not replace finance validation or billing systems.

### 8. Qualification evidence — 1 minute

- **What to click:** Open the persisted 60% and 80% evidence sections.
- **What to explain:** Required questions and evidence govern Proposal and Negotiation progression.
- **Business problem solved:** Prevents a forecast category from being a label without supporting facts.
- **Do not overclaim:** This is deterministic methodology, not AI-generated reasoning.

### 9. Forecast Review — 1.5 minutes

- **What to click:** Close the drawer and open Forecast Review; inspect an existing item without selecting a decision.
- **What to explain:** The manager sees seller classification, evidence, gaps, risks and review history before acting.
- **Business problem solved:** Structures the weekly review one opportunity at a time.
- **Do not overclaim:** Do not click Keep Commit, Move to Upside, Ask Seller or Add Note in the live presentation.

### 10. Ask Seller and response — 1 minute

- **What to click:** Point to an existing question/response in review traceability, if available.
- **What to explain:** Questions are tied to an opportunity, the seller response persists, and the review remains auditable.
- **Business problem solved:** Moves forecast clarification out of ephemeral chat and into the commercial record.
- **Do not overclaim:** Do not create a new question during the demo.

### 11. Alerts — 1 minute

- **What to click:** Open Alerts and inspect one existing signal in read-only mode.
- **What to explain:** Rules identify missing values, stale stages, margin issues, qualification gaps and date inconsistencies.
- **Business problem solved:** Directs attention toward explainable commercial risks.
- **Do not overclaim:** Alerts are deterministic rules, not predictive AI.

### 12. Snapshot and what changed — 1 minute

- **What to click:** Open the forecast/snapshot view and point to existing comparison evidence.
- **What to explain:** Compatible snapshots preserve tenant, team or seller scope and show relevant movement.
- **Business problem solved:** Gives the weekly review a reproducible before/after reference.
- **Do not overclaim:** Do not capture a new snapshot during the presentation.

### 13. Seller Workspace — 1.5 minutes

- **What to click:** Sign out, sign in as Seller, and show the dashboard at desktop or 390 px.
- **What to explain:** Standard shows the full position; Focus prioritizes attention; Guided sequences evidence-backed work.
- **Business problem solved:** Translates management priorities into a seller-owned daily workflow.
- **Do not overclaim:** Recommendations do not autonomously execute sales actions.

### 14. Import workflow — 1 minute

- **What to click:** Return to Tenant Admin and open Import; do not select a file.
- **What to explain:** Files are analyzed, mapped, confirmed and dry-run validated before execution.
- **Business problem solved:** Supports controlled coexistence with CSV/XLSX instead of blindly ingesting spreadsheets.
- **Do not overclaim:** The real customer `Facturado Daily` workbook is still required for customer-specific mapping acceptance.

### 15. Roles and security — 1 minute

- **What to click:** Refer to the profile matrix; optionally show Executive or Viewer without mutation controls.
- **What to explain:** Admin is tenant-wide, Manager is team-scoped, Seller is own-scoped, and Executive/Viewer are read-only according to permissions.
- **Business problem solved:** Preserves responsibility and prevents cross-tenant or cross-team misuse.
- **Do not overclaim:** Enterprise SSO is not included unless separately contracted.

### 16. Roadmap boundary — 1 minute

- **What to click:** Return to the stakeholder review document.
- **What to explain:** Phase 1 is deterministic intelligence. Contextual AI, predictive forecasting, integrations and agents belong to future acceptance decisions.
- **Business problem solved:** Ends the review with a clear delivered-versus-future boundary.
- **Do not overclaim:** Phase 2, Phase 3 and customer production have not started.
